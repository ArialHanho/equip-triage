# Prespecified joint encoder comparison v1

Two already-registered checks compete for the first inspection of one equipment/lot at one cutoff. A registered domain is a candidate fact. Ordered, visible samples and contemporaneous observation notes supply the evidence. The target is the present state of each check and its supporting sample and note. A single continuing check with a cleared counterpart can be released. Unknown, two continuing, two cleared or a structural veto gives hold.

All records are author-generated. Store equipment, lot, lower/upper sequence, observation time, availability time, source IDs and event form. The model receives exactly the normalized complete observation strings, source type, anonymous slot, domain, capped age and sequence offset or note ordinal. Source IDs, equipment/lot identity, family, variant, labels and repair outcomes never enter the tensor stream. The entire evidence sentence must fit 160 UTF-8 bytes. All arms use the same eligibility window, including multilingual WordPiece. BERT receives the same intact strings. No partial-row truncation occurs.

The training, validation and final partitions contain eight, four and six authored bilingual wording families respectively. Every panel, equipment/lot and translation stays within partition. Final also contains two unseen corruptions: simultaneous modality disagreement and a late-arriving current observation with a stale predecessor. Across splits the underlying two-channel mechanism, status vocabulary and synthetic workflow recur. The author created the grammar, rules and challenge. This is a source- and wording-held-out synthetic evaluation.

Use a separate CPU pilot on four training families and two dedicated pilot families, seed 17, two epochs. The pilot has 12 training and eight evaluation panels. It uses fixed threshold 0.50. Final training uses 192 panels, three epochs, logical batch 16, microbatch four, common AdamW batch order and seed 17 on CUDA. Seeds 41 and 73 are prespecified replications. Compare each scheduled seed in full. No checkpoint/epoch selection on final. Native dimensions are 32 with four heads in the row encoder. All native arms have the same modules and total parameter count. Shared supervised arms optimize both candidate state labels, observed-source pointer labels and an auxiliary direct route. Direct-only predicts the first check or hold using route labels alone. For single-modality arms omit the hidden modality from its readout and from its pointer loss. Keep common registry and guard information and publish pre-guard results. A mean-concatenation head, two parallel readers, a memo-conditioned log reader and the same conditioned reader with candidate binding removed are the architectural controls.

The public pretrained comparator is multilingual DistilBERT from revision 45c032ab32cc946ad88a166f7cb282f58c753c2e. It reads identical complete row texts and metadata, uses the conditioned native heads, and fine-tunes the public encoder at 0.00002 and new layers at 0.002. All new native weights use 0.002. Both use FP32, identical logical batches and epochs. Public pretraining, tokenization, parameter count and compute differ. A scoped bilingual rule and character 2–5-gram logistic models using log only, memo only and their interactions are additional comparators.

Select each main arm's release threshold on validation alone from the declared grid. Maximize average English/Korean five-action macro-F1 subject to at most 5% of true holds released in each language. Ties favor lower worst-language false-release rate and then the higher threshold. Always-hold is available when the grid cannot satisfy the constraint. All main calibrations in a seed must exist before final scoring. Also publish fixed-threshold and raw pre-guard metrics. Choose the strongest validation-scoring alternative among rule, fused character model, pooled native, direct-only and pretrained for the primary contrast. Ties use the alphabetical run name.

Success requires positive five-action macro-F1 difference and no increase in false releases among all gold holds in each language against that locked comparator. Report both the observed criterion and the paired family-bootstrap interval criterion. The latter requires lower 95% bound above zero for F1 and upper bound at most zero for false-release difference in both languages. Resample six bilingual families together, 2,000 draws. Compare with every individual arm and disclose strengths of the common guard. Report correctly routed first checks, coverage, wrong-domain decisions, hold errors, three-state macro-F1, learned reference hit at one, null source accuracy and paired counterfactual success. Record CPU/GPU device, parameters, wall time, optimizer steps, seed, hashes, version and source availability. Tiny CPU measurements do not enter the primary contrast.
